# Elevator Pitch

This project analyzed customer churn across telecommunications and retail industries using SQL, MongoDB, exploratory data analysis, and machine learning.

The analysis identified key churn drivers including pricing, tenure, cashback incentives, and customer value.

Decision Tree models achieved the strongest predictive performance, with a best F1 Score of 0.8133.

The project demonstrates how predictive analytics can support proactive customer retention strategies and improve business decision-making.
